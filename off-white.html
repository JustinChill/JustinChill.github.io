Virgil Abloh

Defining the grey area between streetwear and modern design

Off-White is among the most sought-after streetwear brands in the world. Their stark black-and-white, sans-serif logo is a favorite for imitators.

Born outside Chicago in Rockford, Illinois, in 1980, Virgil Abloh was an artist, architect, and fashion designer. After earning a degree in Civil Engineering from the University of Wisconsin–Madison, he obtained a master’s degree in Architecture from the Illinois Institute of Technology. In graduate school, Abloh was introduced to a curriculum established by Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, formed from the notions of the Bauhaus, which taught him to combine the fields of art, craft, and design. These theories, merged with contemporary culture, defined Abloh’s interdisciplinary practice.
