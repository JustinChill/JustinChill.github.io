  780: <h2>300m interns? Jevons paradox at work</h2>
  781: <p>In 1865, William Stanley Jevons first described a paradox. He maintained that more efficient steam
  782: engines would not decrease the use of coal in British factories but would actually increase it. As the
  783: fossil fuel became cheaper, demand for the resource would grow, leading to the construction of more
  784: engines.</p>

  814: <p>The paradox applies to various technological shifts. For example, Los Angeles had 10,000 horses in 1900,
