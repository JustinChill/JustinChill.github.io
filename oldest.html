Supercentenarians

World’s oldest person titleholders since 1955

This dataset is provided by the Gerontology Research Group, a group of researchers that verifies and tracks supercentenarians, or people who are at least 110 years old. This group maintains a list of the verified oldest people in the world. Plotting each person who claimed the title of World’s Oldest Person reveals a few interesting facts about the data:

  • Jeanne Calment (France) held the title for almost 10 years and died at 122, the oldest verified age ever. In 2018, two Russian researchers claimed that her daughter had assumed her identity in 1934, but the researchers who validated her age reviewed the evidence in 2019 and found no sign of fraud, and her record stands.
  • Emma Tillman (U.S.) held the title for just 4 days.
  • Women greatly outnumber men: 63 of the 69 titleholders have been women.
  • Ethel Caterham (U.K.) has held the title since April 2025, and in August 2026 became the first Briton to reach 117.
