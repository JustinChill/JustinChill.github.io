Red Hot Chili Peppers

Choose not a life of imitation

I've just finished reading Scar Tissue by Anthony Kiedis. It's an absolute trip and I couldn't put it down. Kiedis takes us into his world and through his life, laying everything bare and sparing no gory detail. If you haven't had a chance to read it yet, I highly encourage it. Acid for the Children, by Flea, is another great read. Flea writes about his life before the band, including his teenage years in LA with Kiedis. It's a beautiful book. This page is my humble tribute to them and the band that brings so much love to my life.

The Red Hot Chili Peppers were formed in Los Angeles by singer Anthony Kiedis, guitarist Hillel Slovak, bassist Flea, and drummer Jack Irons, classmates at Fairfax High School. Early band names included Tony Flow and the Miraculously Majestic Masters of Mayhem.

Mother's Milk (1989)

Mother's Milk is the fourth studio album by the Red Hot Chili Peppers (RHCP). Released on August 16, 1989, Mother's Milk peaked at number 52 on the U.S. Billboard 200. The single "Knock Me Down" reached number six on the charts; "Higher Ground" peaked at number eleven.

Band members: John Frusciante, Flea, Anthony Kiedis, and Chad Smith.

Blood Sugar Sex Magik (1991)

Blood Sugar Sex Magik is the band's fifth studio album, released on September 24, 1991. Produced by Rick Rubin, the musical style differs notably from the band's previous album Mother's Milk (1989), reducing the use of heavy metal guitar riffs and accentuating the melodic songwriting contributions of guitarist John Frusciante.

Band members: John Frusciante, Flea, Anthony Kiedis, and Chad Smith.

Californication (1999)

Californication, the band's seventh studio album, was released on June 8, 1999, and also produced by Rick Rubin. The album marked the return of John Frusciante, who had previously appeared on Mother's Milk and Blood Sugar Sex Magik, to replace Dave Navarro as the band's guitarist.

Band members: John Frusciante, Flea, Anthony Kiedis, and Chad Smith.

By the Way (2002)

By the Way sold more than 286,000 copies in its first week and peaked at number two on the Billboard 200. The album was released on July 9, 2002.

Singles include "By the Way", "The Zephyr Song", "Can't Stop", and "Universally Speaking".

Band members: John Frusciante, Flea, Anthony Kiedis, and Chad Smith.

Stadium Arcadium (2006)

Stadium Arcadium was released on May 9, 2006. It produced five singles, including "Dani California", "Tell Me Baby", "Snow (Hey Oh)", and "Hump de Bump", along with a fan-made music video for the song "Charlie". In the United States, Stadium Arcadium became the band's first number-one album. It was the group's last to feature guitarist John Frusciante before his departure in 2009 and eventual return in 2019.

Band members: John Frusciante, Flea, Anthony Kiedis, and Chad Smith.

I'm With You (2011)

I'm With You was released on August 29, 2011. The album made its debut at number one in 18 countries including the United Kingdom, while reaching number two in the United States and Canada. Singles include "The Adventures of Rain Dance Maggie" and "Monarchy of Roses".

Band members: Josh Klinghoffer, Anthony Kiedis, Flea, and Chad Smith.

The Getaway (2016)

The Getaway was released on June 17, 2016. Produced by Danger Mouse and mixed by Nigel Godrich, it was the band's first studio album since 1989 not produced by Rick Rubin. The album debuted at number two on the Billboard 200 and topped charts in ten countries. Singles include "Dark Necessities", "Go Robot", "Sick Love", and "Goodbye Angels".

Band members: Josh Klinghoffer, Anthony Kiedis, Flea, and Chad Smith.

Unlimited Love (2022)

Unlimited Love was released on April 1, 2022. It marked the return of longtime guitarist John Frusciante and reunited the band with producer Rick Rubin. The album debuted at number one on the Billboard 200, becoming the band's first US chart-topper since Stadium Arcadium in 2006. Singles include "Black Summer", "These Are the Ways", and "The Shape I'm Takin'".

Band members: John Frusciante, Flea, Anthony Kiedis, and Chad Smith.

Return of the Dream Canteen (2022)

Return of the Dream Canteen followed quickly on October 14, 2022, recorded during the same prolific sessions as Unlimited Love with producer Rick Rubin. The double album debuted at number three on the Billboard 200 and topped the Top Album Sales chart. Singles include "Tippa My Tongue" and "The Drummer".

Band members: John Frusciante, Flea, Anthony Kiedis, and Chad Smith.
