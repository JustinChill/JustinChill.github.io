North American Major League Teams

Tip: Click any node to expand or collapse branches. Shift-click to toggle all sibling branches at once.

Key takeaways:

  • Austin is the largest city (by 2021 population) without a traditional "Big Four" major league sports team (MLB, NBA, NFL, NHL), though it gained MLS's Austin FC in 2021.
  • The smallest cities with major league teams include Green Bay, Fort Lauderdale (home of Inter Miami CF and near the Florida Panthers), and Salt Lake City.
  • Detroit is the smallest city with a professional team in all four major sports leagues.
