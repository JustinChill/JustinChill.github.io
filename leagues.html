North American Sports Leagues

All 154 major league teams, and where they play

Teams borrow the name of the nearest big city, but plenty of them play somewhere else. This is every team in MLB, the NBA, the NFL, the NHL, and MLS, labeled with the place its home field, court, or rink is actually in as of November 2026.

  • Chicago is the only city with a team from all five leagues inside its limits.
  • Add Denver, Detroit, and Philadelphia and you have every city with a team from each of the traditional “Big Four” (MLB, NBA, NFL, and NHL). Detroit, with about 649,000 people, is the smallest of them.
  • Austin, with about 1,003,000 people, is the largest city with no Big Four team of its own or next door, though it has had MLS’s Austin FC since 2021. Fort Worth is a little bigger and has no team at all, but Dallas and Arlington are its neighbors.
  • The smallest homes are stadium towns. East Rutherford, N.J., has about 10,600 people and two NFL teams, and Foxborough, Mass., has about 18,900 people, the Patriots, and the Revolution. Green Bay, at about 107,000, is the smallest city that isn’t a suburb of a bigger one.

Footnotes

  1. U.S. Census Bureau, City and Town Population Totals: 2020–2025. All of the populations here are its estimates for July 1, 2025. ↩
