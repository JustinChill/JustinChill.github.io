Stages of the Corona Era

The most important events of the COVID-19 pandemic against the backdrop of the number of deaths worldwide and the number of vaccine doses administered.

The toll, year by year

Reported COVID-19 deaths passed seven million in November 2023. The deadliest stretch came in January 2021, before most of the world could get a vaccine, when the seven-day average peaked at nearly 15,000 deaths a day. That made 2021 the deadliest year, with about 3.6 million reported deaths, almost twice as many as in 2020.

2021 was also the year of the vaccine. More than nine billion doses were given that year, peaking at about 44 million a day at the end of June, and the world passed 13 billion doses in November 2022. Reported deaths fell as immunity built up, to 1.3 million in 2022, about 310,000 in 2023 and 65,000 in 2024. Those are only the deaths that were recorded: the World Health Organization estimates that the pandemic caused about 14.9 million excess deaths in 2020 and 2021 alone.
