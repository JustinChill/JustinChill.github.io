Video-game consoles

The generation game

From the first generation of home video-game consoles to the eighth, each generation outsold the one before it. The first machines, such as the 1972 Magnavox Odyssey, sold 4.5m units between them; the eighth, led by the Nintendo Switch and PlayStation 4, sold 346m. This chart recreates a 2013 graphic from The Economist that lines up each console by the year it launched, and extends it to 2026.

Eight generations, each bigger than the last

Selected consoles, lifetime units sold, m

The PlayStation 2 is still the best-selling console, at more than 160m, but the original Switch is only 2.8m behind it at 157.2m, and the eighth generation it led is the biggest yet. Microsoft, which no longer reports console sales, peaked with the Xbox 360 at 84m; its successors sold an estimated 58m and 35.5m. The ninth generation is still on sale: the PlayStation 5 passed 100m in November 2026, six years after launch, and the Switch 2 sold 26.35m in its first 16 months.
