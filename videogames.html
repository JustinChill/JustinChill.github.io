Video-game consoles

Selected consoles, lifetime units sold, m

From the first generation of home video-game consoles to the seventh, each generation outsold the one before it. The first machines, such as the 1972 Magnavox Odyssey, sold 4.5m units between them; the seventh generation of the Xbox 360, Wii and PlayStation 3 sold 273m. This chart recreates a 2013 graphic from The Economist that lines up each console by the year it launched.
