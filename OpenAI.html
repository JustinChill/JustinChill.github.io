OpenAI timeline

From Sam to Sora

OpenAI milestones, 2015–2026

OpenAI was founded in December 2015 by Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, Ilya Sutskever, Wojciech Zaremba, and John Schulman, along with Andrej Karpathy, Durk Kingma, Trevor Blackwell, Vicki Cheung and Pamela Vagata. It was established as a non-profit research organization to promote and develop friendly AI for the benefit of humanity. In 2019, OpenAI created a “capped-profit” company, OpenAI LP, under the non-profit OpenAI Inc., to better fund and scale its research. In October 2025 it restructured again: the for-profit arm became OpenAI Group PBC, a public benefit corporation, controlled by the non-profit, now called the OpenAI Foundation, which holds about a quarter of it.

Key People

  • Elon Musk: Co-founder who provided initial backing before stepping down from the board in 2018 to avoid potential conflicts with Tesla’s AI initiatives.
  • Sam Altman: Initially co-chairman alongside Musk, Altman later became CEO, steering OpenAI’s transition into a commercial powerhouse and leading its strategic partnerships. The board briefly removed him in November 2023 before reinstating him days later.
  • Greg Brockman: Co-founder and President, Brockman has played an instrumental role in building the engineering infrastructure and core project teams.
  • Ilya Sutskever: Co-founder and former Chief Scientist whose pioneering research in deep learning set OpenAI’s technical direction for landmark models. He left in 2024 to found Safe Superintelligence.
  • Wojciech Zaremba: Co-founder who led OpenAI’s robotics and code generation research tracks.
  • John Schulman: Co-founder who created the Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) algorithm and led the reinforcement learning work behind ChatGPT, which used reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF). He left in 2024.
  • Mira Murati: Former Chief Technology Officer who oversaw the deployment of ChatGPT and DALL·E, briefly serving as interim CEO during the November 2023 leadership crisis. She left in 2024 and founded Thinking Machines Lab.

Contributions and Projects

OpenAI is known for breakthrough contributions across machine learning, robotics, and generative AI safety. Notable projects include:

  • GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) Series: Autoregressive language models capable of generating human-like text, code, and analysis. GPT-3, GPT-4 and GPT-5 set the standard for modern foundation models, and the series powers ChatGPT, launched in November 2022.
  • OpenAI Five: A reinforcement learning system that defeated the reigning world champion esports team in the complex multiplayer game Dota 2.
  • DALL·E: Deep learning models that synthesize photorealistic and stylized images from natural language prompts.

Mission and Vision

OpenAI’s mission is centered on ensuring that artificial general intelligence (AGI)—highly autonomous systems that outperform humans at most economically valuable work—benefits all of humanity.

From early reinforcement learning benchmarks to multimodal foundation models, OpenAI continues to sit at the forefront of AI research, shaping how machine learning technologies are built and deployed globally.
