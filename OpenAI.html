OpenAI timeline

From Sam to Sora

Artificial intelligence

OpenAI was founded in December 2015 by Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, Ilya Sutskever, Wojciech Zaremba, and John Schulman. It was established as a non-profit research organization to promote and develop friendly AI for the benefit of humanity. In 2019, OpenAI transitioned into a “capped-profit” company, OpenAI LP, under the umbrella of the non-profit OpenAI Inc., to better fund and scale its research efforts.

Key People

  • Elon Musk: Co-founder who provided initial backing before stepping down from the board in 2018 to avoid potential conflicts with Tesla's AI initiatives.
  • Sam Altman: Initially co-chairman alongside Musk, Altman later became CEO, steering OpenAI's transition into a commercial powerhouse and leading its strategic partnerships.
  • Greg Brockman: Co-founder and President, Brockman has played an instrumental role in building the engineering infrastructure and core project teams.
  • Ilya Sutskever: Co-founder and former Chief Scientist whose pioneering research in deep learning set OpenAI's technical direction for landmark models.
  • Wojciech Zaremba: Co-founder who led OpenAI's robotics and code generation research tracks.
  • John Schulman: Co-founder and lead architect behind Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), core techniques that enabled ChatGPT.
  • Mira Murati: Former Chief Technology Officer who oversaw the deployment of ChatGPT and DALL·E, briefly serving as interim CEO during the November 2023 leadership crisis.

Contributions and Projects

OpenAI is known for breakthrough contributions across machine learning, robotics, and generative AI safety. Notable projects include:

  • GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) Series: Autoregressive language models capable of generating human-like text, code, and analysis, with models like GPT-3 and GPT-4 establishing modern foundation model standards.
  • OpenAI Five: A reinforcement learning system that defeated the reigning world champion esports team in the complex multiplayer game Dota 2.
  • DALL·E: Deep learning models that synthesize photorealistic and stylized images from natural language prompts.

Mission and Vision

OpenAI's mission is centered on ensuring that artificial general intelligence (AGI)—highly autonomous systems that outperform humans at most economically valuable work—benefits all of humanity.

From early reinforcement learning benchmarks to multimodal foundation models, OpenAI continues to sit at the forefront of AI research, shaping how machine learning technologies are built and deployed globally.
