8 minutes, 46 seconds

8:46

On May 25, 2020, Derek Chauvin held his knee on the neck of George Floyd for 8 minutes and 46 seconds, resulting in Mr. Floyd’s death.

I invite you to sit quietly and reflect for this same length of time. It feels like an eternity. If you feel discomfort, and want to leave the website prior to the timer finishing, reflect on that feeling.

8 minutes and 46 seconds was the time given in the original criminal complaint. At Chauvin’s 2021 trial, prosecutors showed that he knelt on Mr. Floyd’s neck for 9 minutes and 29 seconds.
