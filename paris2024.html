Paris 2024 Olympic Medals

The U.S.A. and China share the title for most gold medals

Sort by

The United States and China each won 40 gold medals in Paris, the first tie for the most golds at a Summer Olympics. On total medals it wasn’t close: 126 to 91. Britain came third with 65, one more than France, the host.

Strictly speaking, athletes compete for a National Olympic Committee, and each committee has a three-letter code. Some take no thought, like USA and FRA. Others need a second look: SUI is Switzerland, from the French Suisse, and BRN is Bahrain. Brunei is BRU.

Two codes in Paris belonged to no country. EOR, from its name in French, was the Refugee Olympic Team. AIN was the 32 Russians and Belarusians let in as Individual Neutral Athletes. They won five medals (one gold, three silver and a bronze), which the IOC leaves out of its table, so they aren’t in the chart either.

First-ever medals and golds

Albania, Cape Verde, Dominica and Saint Lucia all won an Olympic medal for the first time, and so did the Refugee Olympic Team. Dominica and Saint Lucia went straight to gold. Botswana and Guatemala had medals already, but never a gold until Paris.
